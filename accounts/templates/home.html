Understanding Sessions

Every time you log in, a new session is created. This session holds your authentication state and preferences.

Sessions are stored server-side and linked to a unique identifier sent to your browser as a cookie. This ensures that only you can access your protected resources. You can end a session by logging out or after a period of inactivity.

Login Security

Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text. We use industry‑standard encryption.

Additional measures like rate limiting and CSRF tokens protect against brute‑force and cross‑site request forgery attacks. Always use strong, unique passwords and enable two‑factor authentication if available.
